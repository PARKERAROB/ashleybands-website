Student Expectations

Spring Trip 2026 · read on the bus before departure
Hub Bus Checkoff Check-In Instructions

This is a school trip, not a private vacation. Today and tomorrow work if we do three things:

  1. Stay accounted for.
  2. Communicate quickly.
  3. Make good decisions when we are tired.

You will have freedom in the park. That freedom depends on check-ins, staying in groups, and responding immediately. If we have to look for you, your group's freedom changes. Take care of each other, represent the band well, and make the easy decisions easy.

The Non-Negotiables

  • Groups of 3 or more at all times in the park. No singles. No pairs.
  • Your assigned chaperone does not change, even if your group does.
  • Group changes only happen if they are clearly communicated to your chaperone first.
  • Respond to texts and calls from adults immediately.
  • Stay with your room group. Get your key from an adult. You don't leave your room unless an adult says so.

Check-In Procedure

Both steps or it doesn't count.

  1. Text your assigned chaperone: group photo with faces visible, names of everyone, current location.
  2. Post in the student Google Chat after the chaperone confirms.
"Group 6 check-in: Ava, Jayden, Marcus, Lily. At Italy bridge. All good."

No sunglasses-only mystery photos. Faces visible.

At The Performance Site Tonight

Late. Tired. Another school's building. Our job is to be the easiest group they deal with tonight.

  • Quiet voices.
  • Stay together.
  • No wandering.
  • No hallway playing.
  • Bathroom only with permission and in groups.
  • Go directly where you're told.
  • Don't touch anything that isn't ours.

At The Hotel

  • Stay seated on the bus until told.
  • Don't flood the lobby.
  • Stay with your room group.
  • Get your key from an adult.
  • Text proof-of-life from inside your room.
  • Door latches checked.
  • Devices charging.
  • You know wake-up time and departure time.
  • You do not leave your room without permission.

At The Park Tomorrow

  • Groups of 3+. No exceptions.
  • Respond to communication immediately.
  • Every check-in on time.
  • Do not leave the park.
  • Do not enter restricted areas, employee areas, or parking lots.
  • Communicate any group change before it happens.

Consequences

Practical, not dramatic. If we have to look for you, you get attached to an adult.

Step 1. Not following expectations → you lose independence and stay with a chaperone for the rest of the day.
Step 2. More serious issues → you stay directly with Mr. Parker for the rest of the trip.
Step 3. Severe issues → your family is contacted and we figure out next steps together.

If Something Is Off

If someone in your group is sick, missing, separated, upset, injured, or not responding — tell an adult immediately. Don't wait and see. Don't assume someone else has it.

Take care of each other.
