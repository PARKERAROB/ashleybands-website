Student Check-In Instructions

Ashley Bands Spring Trip · Busch Gardens
Trip Hub

Check-In Window

Saturday, 12:00-12:30 PM.

Check-in is not complete until both steps happen.

How To Check In

  1. Take one group photo with every group member's face visible.
  2. Text the photo to your assigned chaperone.
  3. Include every student's name and your current location.
  4. Post the same check-in to Google Chat.
  5. Wait for confirmation from your chaperone.

Example: Group 6 check-in: Ava, Jayden, Marcus, Lily. At Italy bridge. All good.

Rules That Matter

  • Groups of 3 or more at all times.
  • No one goes anywhere alone.
  • Respond immediately to adult texts/calls.
  • Group changes require clear communication.
  • If someone is sick, missing, separated, upset, or not responding, tell an adult immediately.
