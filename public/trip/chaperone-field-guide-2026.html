Proof-Of-Life Check-In

  • Check-in window: Saturday 12:00-12:30 PM.
  • Student/group sends photo to assigned chaperone.
  • Photo must show all group members' faces clearly.
  • Text must include all names and current location.
  • After texting chaperone, group posts the same check-in to Google Chat.
  • Chaperone confirms receipt.
  • At 12:30 PM, begin contacting any group that has not checked in.

Example: Group 6 check-in: Ava, Jayden, Marcus, Lily. At Italy bridge. All good.

Missing / Late / Not Responding

  1. Text and call the student/group lead.
  2. Text/call another student in that group.
  3. Tell Rob immediately.
  4. Do not assume another adult handled it.
  5. Keep trying until Rob confirms the next step.

Sick / Injured / Upset Student

  • Stay calm and keep the student with an adult.
  • Tell Rob immediately.
  • Use the medical form source of truth when needed.
  • For anything urgent, call emergency services first, then Rob.
  • Do not distribute detailed medical information broadly.

Hotel Procedures

  • Students stay seated or in the assigned area until dismissed.
  • One adult checks in; one adult supervises students.
  • One adult handles room keys; one adult manages luggage flow if available.
  • Students stay with room group and do not flood the lobby.
  • Students receive keys/instructions from adults.
  • Students text proof-of-life from inside room.
  • Chaperones conduct hallway/door checks.

Park Procedures

  • Groups of 3 or more at all times.
  • No student may be alone.
  • Chaperone assignments do not change.
  • Group changes require clear communication and confirmation by group lead and assigned chaperone.
  • Awards are at 3:30 PM at the Globe Theater.
  • Depart park at 5:30 PM.
  • If a student misses check-in or causes adults to search, independent park freedom ends.
