Paying toward your student’s trip

  1. Open your student’s music notes link, in the Family Portal or on their QR card.
  2. Choose “Our family’s payment toward your student’s trip.”
  3. Pick an amount and add your name.
  4. Pay online, or choose Pay by check.
Check or cash · no feePayable to Ashley Band Boosters. Put your student’s name and “Carnegie family payment” on the memo or envelope. Bring it to the band room.
