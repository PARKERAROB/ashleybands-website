Robert A. Parker, conductor

Part IV

Wind Ensemble

Cenotaph

Fanfare for Band

Jack Stamp

A cenotaph is a monument to a person who is buried somewhere else. Jack Stamp wrote this fanfare in 1992 for the Southern California All-State Band.

The Star-Spangled Banner

John Stafford Smith  /  arr. Jack Stamp

Jack Stamp wrote this arrangement as a hymn setting of our national anthem, reflecting the fourth verse of Francis Scott Key's poem. Please remain seated and enjoy it as a concert piece.

The Entertainer

Scott Joplin  /  arr. Bill Holcombe and Carson Rothrock

Scott Joplin published this rag in 1902. It found a huge new audience when it became the heart of the soundtrack for the 1973 film The Sting.

A Bernstein Tribute

Leonard Bernstein  /  adapted by Clare Grundman

Clare Grundman's 1991 medley visits three of Leonard Bernstein's stage works: West Side Story (Prologue, Somewhere, Scherzo and Mambo), On the Town (The Great Lover, Times Square: 1944 and Lonely Town), and the overture to Candide.
