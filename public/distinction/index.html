Active musical opportunities across the year

Pageantry / Athletic Bands ✓

The Screaming Eagle Regiment performed a full competitive marching season (Northside, Topsail, and the 24th Annual Crystal Coast Band Classic) plus the home and in-county football schedule.

At the Crystal Coast Band Classic (Nov 1, 2025): 2nd place, Class AA · 88.39 · 10th overall, with caption honors in Visual Performance, General Effect, and Drum Major.

Solo / Small Ensembles & Chamber ✓

The Spring Concert featured a student soloist and several student small ensembles — including one composed entirely of seniors — that were fully student-directed, rehearsed and prepared without staff conducting. A standing Percussion Ensemble performed on all three concerts, and the jazz combo performed in the community. Ashley students also played the pit orchestra for the school musical Big Fish (April 24).

Jazz Band ✓

An active, self-directed jazz combo performing a working book of standards — featured at the Spring Concert (May 12) and in the community for the A4LE gathering at Wrightsville Beach (May 4).

Combo book: Autumn Leaves · Blue Bossa · There Will Never Be Another You · St. Thomas · Fly Me to the Moon · All of Me · Take the A Train · It Could Happen to You · Girl from Ipanema · The Days of Wine and Roses · Blue Monk · My Little Suede Shoes · Recordame · Satin Doll · Four · Caravan · Black Orpheus · Misty.

Extending music into the community

Conductor's Clinic & Concert Weekend Feb 27–28, 2026

Ashley hosted the Murray Middle School bands and brought in three guest clinicians (Price, Tyson, Pellas) who rotated through all three ensembles. The weekend also featured a 25th-Anniversary Alumni Band (rehearsal + lunch) and a Wilmington Symphonic Winds concert at Ashley in collaboration with Ashley Bands — capped by a joint concert at MEAC.

CCC Weekend Schedule WSW “American Dances”

Music Education Advocacy May 20, 2026

Ashley students traveled to Raleigh to meet with North Carolina legislators and speak to the value of music education and public-school support.

Legislative visit brief

Students in community & university ensembles

Ashley students were invited and selected to perform with the Wilmington Symphonic Winds — the region's premier community wind band — at both the "American Dances" concert (Feb 28, at Ashley) and the "TITANS" concert (May 31). Students also join the UNCW Summer Concert Band, a university recruitment ensemble (concluding concert June 15, 2026).

Hosted & ran the District Concert MPA — South Site

March 2026 · Ashley HS / MEAC

Ashley was the host site for the NCBA Eastern District Concert MPA, and the band program ran the event end to end — stage crew, emcee, food, band flow, and the sight-reading room. The event ran on a web app built in-house by Ashley's director, with volunteer signups coordinated by Mrs. Meade.

Junction Bowl

Oct 15, 2025

A football game between the two feeder middle schools, Murray and Myrtle Grove. Ashley Bands were invited, and several Ashley students performed alongside the middle school musicians — supporting the feeder programs the high school grows from.

Murray MS jazz collaboration

Winter & Spring

Murray Middle School invites Ashley high-school students to perform with its jazz band before Murray's Winter and Spring concerts — a recurring mentorship that strengthens the feeder pipeline.

Joined the New Hanover HS Band

Oct 17, 2025

At the away football game against New Hanover, Ashley joined forces with the New Hanover band to support their program — one local high-school band lifting another.

A4LE Community Performance

May 4, 2026

Jazz combo performed for a regional gathering of school-facility planners and architects at Wrightsville Beach.
