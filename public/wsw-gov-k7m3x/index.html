Wilmington Symphonic Winds

Governance Refresh — a concept for your input

A short overview for Gwen and Dachuan, from Rob · June 2026

This is an idea and a shape, not a set of motions — I wanted to show you the whole picture before building anything out, and hear whether you think it is a sound direction.

Why now

As we move through this transition, I have been looking back over our first ten or eleven years and reading our governing documents against what WSW actually does today. A few good updates would bring the documents into better alignment with how the ensemble really operates, and let us retire some early founding-era language that no longer fits. Mostly, this is about bringing our paperwork up to speed with what is already happening — and putting the organization on a footing that does not depend on any one person.

The direction

A refreshed sense of who we are: a leading wind band of the Cape Fear region that performs exceptional music at a professional artistic level, connects meaningfully with our audiences through free and welcoming performances, and strengthens the region's musical life by uniting student, collegiate, and professional musicians.

Artistic Excellencethe finest repertoire, performed at the highest level

What would change at the top

  • Update our stated mission and purpose to the language above.
  • We keep our five officers (President, President-Elect, Secretary, Treasurer, and Administrative Coordinator), with each now anchoring a clear operating area. The Administrative Coordinator role is refreshed as the administrative engine of our community and outreach work, and the Past President continues as an ex-officio advisor and continuity voice.
  • Artistic leadership defined in our documents: an Artistic Director role that can be held by one person or split between an Artistic Director for Internal Operations and one for External Operations, each a contracted, non-voting position. On concert programs, both are simply "Artistic Director."
  • A small decision triangle — the President plus the two directors — to resolve day-to-day operational questions and keep us aligned.

What would NOT change

Our Articles of Incorporation, our 501(c)(3) status, and our conflict-of-interest policy all stay as they are. These are bylaws-level updates the board can adopt by vote — not a charter rewrite.

One housekeeping item

Separately: our registered agent on file with the State is Tom Smicklas, who has passed away, so that needs updating regardless. I would suggest attaching that role to the Treasurer's office going forward, so it is always owned by a seat.

The ask

Does this direction make sense to you? If you like it, I will flesh it out all the way into the full language for the board to review and adopt. I would love your thoughts, and I am happy to talk it through anytime.

— Rob
