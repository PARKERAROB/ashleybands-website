## Program Overview

**Eugene Ashley High School Band Program**
555 Halyburton Memorial Parkway · Wilmington, NC 28412
Phone: (910) 790-2360

Nearly 100 student musicians per semester.
2025 marks the 25th Anniversary of music at Ashley High School.

**Mission:** Two-part: (1) Empowering students to reach their full potential as individual musicians. (2) Providing a band program that serves the needs of our students, our school, and our community. Progress and achievement depend on working together in an organized and systematic way. Each member relies on the others for high musical and moral standards, strong character, and a sense of responsibility toward the group.

**Mottos:** *Unus pro omnibus, omnes pro uno* (One for all, all for one) · *E pluribus unum* (Out of many, one)

**Ensembles:** Concert Bands · Marching Band (Screaming Eagle Regiment) · Percussion Ensemble · Jazz Band · Colorguard · Pep Band

Ashley proudly hosts the **NCBA Eastern District MPA South** each March.

## Concert Attire — Musician Black

All students wear all-black for concerts. No colors, logos, or patterns.

- **Tops:** All-black shirts/blouses/dresses. No sleeveless, strapless, halter, or crop tops. Cap, 3/4, or full-length sleeves.
- **Bottoms:** Solid black dress pants/slacks or knee-length+ skirts/dresses. No jeans, leggings, yoga pants, sweatpants, or shorts.
- **Footwear:** Black dress shoes or flats. No sneakers, flip-flops, or athletic shoes.
- **Socks/tights:** Must be black. No bare legs or colored hosiery.
- **Accessories/jewelry:** Minimal, non-reflective. Nothing shiny under stage lights.
