# Family Sponsorship

## Support Your Student. Support the Band.

Like many public school arts programs, we rely on the generous support of our families and community to meet the financial needs of the Ashley High School Band Program. Your donation directly supports every student's experience by providing high-quality instruction, performance opportunities, and the resources that make our band program possible.

## Why Your Support Matters

Band students represent the best of Ashley High School. They:

- invest hundreds of hours in rehearsal and performance
- develop discipline, teamwork, and leadership
- consistently demonstrate strong academic achievement

Your contribution helps us:

- provide quality uniforms and attire
- keep students looking sharp on the field and stage through maintenance and replacement of essential performance wear
- hire skilled instructors and clinicians
- purchase and maintain essential equipment
- repair aging instruments and replace equipment to ensure longevity and quality
- cover travel and event expenses

## Screaming Eagle Family Giving Levels

### Band Friend - $50

- Family name listed in concert programs and on the band website
- Certificate of appreciation

### Band Supporter - $100

All Friend benefits, plus:

- Ashley Band supporter shirt, if received by August 5

### Band Patron - $250

All Supporter benefits, plus:

- Framed band photo
- Recognition on the Ashley Band room donor wall

### Band Champion - $500

All Patron benefits, plus:

- Student thank-you card signed by the band

### Legacy Family - $1,000+

All Champion benefits, plus:

- Personalized legacy plaque displayed in the band room
- Recognition at the annual Coastal Concert & Clinic performance
- Dedicated thank-you post on Ashley Bands social media pages
