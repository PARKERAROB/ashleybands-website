# Corporate Sponsorship

Like many public school arts programs, we rely on the generous support of our community and our Band Booster organization to meet the financial needs of the program. Your sponsorship ensures that Ashley band students receive the best music education and performance opportunities possible.

## Why Your Support Matters

Band students embody the best qualities we hope to see in young people. They:

- invest hundreds of hours in rehearsal and performance
- develop discipline, teamwork, and leadership
- consistently demonstrate strong academic achievement

Your contribution helps us:

- provide quality uniforms and attire
- replace and maintain uniforms and performance wear so every student looks and feels their best on the field and on stage
- hire skilled instructors, clinicians, and specialists
- purchase and maintain essential equipment
- repair aging instruments and begin replacing key equipment to keep the program strong and sustainable
- cover travel expenses for competitions and events

## Screaming Eagle Sponsor Levels

### Friend of the Band - $100

- Listing in concert programs and on the band website
- Sponsor shirt, if received by August 5
- Certificate of appreciation and tax receipt

### Patron of the Band - $500

All Friend benefits, plus:

- Framed band photo
- Business logo in concert programs

### Community Sponsor - $750

All Patron benefits, plus:

- Band banner displayed with our ensemble
- Student thank-you letter
- Increased public address recognition at events

### Premier Sponsor - $1,250

All Community benefits, plus:

- Band apparel, 2 items
- Digital concert program ad

### Legacy Sponsor - $5,000

All Premier benefits, plus:

- Additional band apparel, 4 items total
- Recognition at our annual Coastal Concert & Clinic performance
- Dedicated social media thank-you post
- Custom Legacy Plaque recognizing long-term impact on the Ashley Band Program
