## Use Your Student's Personal Link

The general campaign page is useful, but each student has a personal fundraising link.

Families should share the student's personal link whenever possible.

That is where the fundraising actually connects back to the student.

## Who To Share With

This fundraiser works best when families share beyond the immediate household.

Good people to share with:

- grandparents
- aunts and uncles
- family friends
- neighbors
- former Ashley Band families
- local supporters
- anyone who wants to support music education at Ashley

## Why This Matters

The band program has real costs across the full year:

- student travel
- instruments and equipment
- repairs
- instructional support
- event costs
- marching band needs
- concert band needs

Fundraising helps reduce the direct burden on families and makes more opportunities possible for students.
