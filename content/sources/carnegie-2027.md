## Carnegie Hall 2027

Ashley Bands is planning a New York trip for March 23-26, 2027, with the Carnegie Hall performance on March 25, 2027. Final participation, price, approvals, and funding remain subject to confirmation.

### Where things stand

The trip deposit has been submitted. The plan is to take everyone.

### Help raise the money

Students can share our story through My Carnegie Notes and letters in the [Family Portal](/portal). Every gift lowers the trip cost for every student who goes. Anyone can give at [Support the Carnegie trip](/support-carnegie).

### Cost and payments

Each student's share is **$2,000**. The first payment is **$650, our goal by Friday, October 30**, and the $50 deposit counts toward it. The trip needs it for our first group payment, but it is not required: no student loses their place for not paying by Friday. Gifts given for your student through Music Notes count toward their share.

Pay in the [Family Portal](/portal) under **Payments**: check or cash to Ashley Band Boosters (best), or online. Details, deadlines, and adult options are on [Carnegie questions and answers](/info/carnegie-questions).

### Refunds

There is no refund protection on this trip, for the group or for families. Once a traveler is registered with WorldStrides, its cancellation terms apply. Swapping a traveler's name after registration is allowed. Adult travel payments are non-refundable.
