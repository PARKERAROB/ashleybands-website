## Do these three things

- Open the Perry's Gourmet Popcorn store.
- Choose **Ashley HS Band** as the group.
- Enter the student's full name in the optional seller note at checkout.

**[Shop the popcorn fundraiser](https://www.perrysgourmetpopcornfundraising.com/)**

## The biggest help is sharing

Send the store link to grandparents, relatives, family friends, neighbors, and coworkers. Share it on social media too. A personal message from a student or family is more useful than a general post by itself.

### Short message to share

Ashley Bands' popcorn fundraiser is open through midnight Wednesday, September 9. Choose Ashley HS Band at checkout and add the student's full name in the optional seller note. Shop here: https://www.perrysgourmetpopcornfundraising.com/

## What happens after an order

- Ashley Bands receives 40% of every online order.
- Perry's handles payment and ships directly to the purchaser within the lower 48 states.
- Students do not collect money, sort orders, or make deliveries.
- Student credit may be applied to an applicable Ashley Bands funding goal, including marching band or the Carnegie Hall trip.
