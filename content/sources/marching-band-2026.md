## What a Competitive Season Requires

- consistent attendance
- summer preparation
- field rehearsal time
- instructional and design staff
- transportation, equipment, and logistics
- family and booster support

Success for us is not only trophies. It is students who are prepared, supported, and performing at the highest level they are capable of.

## The Real Cost

We believe in being honest about cost up front.

The full competitive season costs roughly **$42,000 to $47,000** to operate. That covers staff, custom music, drill and choreography, colorguard uniforms and equipment, props, instruments and equipment, transportation, competition logistics, meals, water, and maintenance.

NHCS has covered transportation for the past two seasons, and we expect that to continue. If it does, the shared funding goal for the band is closer to **$36,000 to $41,000**.

On a projected 60-student band, that is about **$600 to $685 per student** in true per-pupil cost.

That number is not a bill. No student pays a required individual fee to participate. See [how we fund the season](/info/marching-band-funding) for the full breakdown.

## How We Fund It, and Why Everyone Helps

We set Fair-Share Funding Goals and ask every family to help meet them. The most important way is sponsorships. Regardless of whether your student marches, we encourage every family to try to land a sponsor. One $2,000 sponsor can cover a student and several others.

Funding comes from:

- sponsorships (the path we lean on hardest)
- fundraisers
- booster support
- school and district support where available
- optional family contributions
- hardship support, always

No student is excluded from a band activity because of financial hardship. If cost is a concern, talk to Mr. Parker. That conversation is always available and always private.

## Signing Day - June 1

**You must complete the sign-up form to finalize your spot in marching band.** Sign-up closes on **June 1**.

[Complete the 2026 Marching Band Sign-Up Form](/marching-band-signup-2026)

At Signing Day, each family chooses one path:

- **Path A:** $500 up front
- **Path B:** $250 up front, then $250 by band camp
- **Path C:** Raise $500 through sponsorships and/or fundraising. Many families land a $2,000+ sponsor and are effectively done, covering their student and others. Start here: [Family Campaign Tools](/sponsors/campaign) walk you through it and give you a tracker.
- **Path D:** Talk with Mr. Parker. If none of the above works, we build a path that does. No pushback, no student left out.

If the form is not completed by June 1, we will assume the student is not marching this season. Mr. Parker or student leadership may still reach out to confirm.
