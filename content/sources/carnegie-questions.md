## Carnegie Hall 2027: your questions

Both of Ashley's concert bands, the Concert Band and the Wind Ensemble, perform at Carnegie Hall in New York on Thursday, March 25, 2027. We travel March 23-26. These are the questions families ask most. Each answer reflects what we know as of October 27, 2026, and we'll update this page as answers come in.

### Why does this trip matter?

Most musicians never get to play on that stage. Our students earned it, and the plan is to take everyone who wants to go. Every gift lowers the cost for every student.

### How much will it cost my family?

Thanks to everyone's fundraising, the trip is already under $2,000 per traveler, and we're still working to bring it lower for everyone.

- **If you're able,** please pay your student's full trip now ($2,000 at most).
- **If you can't,** the goal for the first payment is $650.
- **If that's not possible either,** pay what you can toward it. Every bit counts.

Your $50 deposit already counts toward your payment. If cost is a barrier for your family, [contact Mr. Parker](mailto:robert.parker@nhcs.net). It stays private.

### When are the payments and deadlines?

- **Friday, October 30:** the first family payment (see above).
- **Group payments:** the Boosters make the group payments to WorldStrides, our tour company. The next one is November 1, and the final one is due January 7, 2027.
- **Registration:** families will register each traveler in the WorldStrides portal. WorldStrides' deadline is December 13. We'll tell you when it opens.
- **After you register,** WorldStrides' cancellation terms apply: $225 through November 22, then 25% of the trip price, 50% from January 7, and no refund from February 7.

### How do I pay?

Sign in to the [Family Portal](/portal) and open My Carnegie Notes for your student. Choose "Our family's payment toward" your student's trip and pay with PayPal. Or send a check to Ashley Band Boosters with your student's name on it. Checks have no fee. A family payment is not tax-deductible.

### If we pay, do we still need to fundraise?

Yes, please. Paying your share is step one. It holds your student's spot. Fundraising is how we bring every kid with us. About half our band needs some help to make this trip, and we go as a band. Your fundraising still counts after you pay: every dollar lowers the cost for everyone.

### Can I chaperone?

The trip has 10 adults: two directors and 8 chaperones. Chaperones pay $2,000. If you're interested, [contact Mr. Parker](mailto:robert.parker@nhcs.net).

### Can family come to New York?

Yes. Parents and family can come on their own and get themselves to New York and back. We're getting the price and sign-up details from WorldStrides and will post them here.

### How do family tickets to the concert work?

Every traveler on the trip has a concert ticket. Family not on the trip buy tickets from the Carnegie Hall box office about six weeks before the concert, around mid-February. The festival guide lists them at $90.

### How does rooming work?

Students room 4 to a room, boys with boys and girls with girls. Adults room 2 to a room. The directors are in single rooms.

1. **Get 10 yeses of $25.** A gift or a trade: wash a car, walk a dog, draw a picture.
2. **Share your student's link.** It's first in the [Family Portal](/portal) under My Carnegie Notes.
3. **Donate a raffle prize** for the drawing at the Winter Concert on Tuesday, December 8. [Offer a prize](/raffle).
