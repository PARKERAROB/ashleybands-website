## Carnegie Hall 2027: your questions

Both of Ashley's concert bands, the Concert Band and the Wind Ensemble, perform at Carnegie Hall in New York on Thursday, March 25, 2027. We travel March 23-26. These are the questions families ask most. Each answer reflects what we know as of October 27, 2026, and we'll update this page as answers come in.

### Why does this trip matter?

Most musicians never get to play on that stage. Our students earned it, and the plan is to take everyone who wants to go. Every gift lowers the cost for every student.

### How much will it cost my family?

Each student's share is **$2,000**. Gifts given for your student through their Music Notes link count toward it, and every general gift lowers the cost for every student. Anything credited above $2,000 goes to the whole trip fund. If you're worried about the cost, [talk to Mr. Parker](mailto:robert.parker@nhcs.net).

A family's payment toward a student's trip is not a charitable gift and is not tax-deductible.

### When are the payments and deadlines?

- **First payment: $650, our goal by Friday, October 30.** The $50 deposit counts toward it. The trip needs this money for our first group payment, but it is not required: no student loses their place for not paying by Friday. Pay what you can, when you can.
- **The rest:** pay any amount, any time, toward your student's $2,000 share. The final group payment to WorldStrides, our tour company, is due January 7, 2027.
- **Registration:** we start registering travelers with WorldStrides as payments start and students have funds in hand. WorldStrides' deadline is December 13. We'll tell you when it's your student's turn.
- **No refund protection.** Neither the group nor families have trip insurance through WorldStrides. Swapping a traveler's name after registration is allowed.
- **After you register,** WorldStrides' cancellation terms apply: $225 through November 22, then 25% of the trip price, 50% from January 7, and no refund from February 7.

### How do I pay?

Sign in to the [Family Portal](/portal) and choose **Payments**. You'll see your student's share, gifts given for them, what you've paid, and what's still owed.

- **Check or cash (best, every dollar counts).** Make checks payable to **Ashley Band Boosters**, with "Carnegie" and your student's name in the memo line, and turn it in at the band room. Then click **Record a check or cash I turned in**.
- **Online.** Click **Pay online**. PayPal keeps a small fee; you can add it so the full amount counts.

Music Notes is for supporters' gifts. Please make your family's own payments under Payments.

### Can I chaperone?

The trip has 10 official adult places: two directors and 8 chaperones, and only 3 chaperone places remain. Official chaperones pay **$2,500** per person in a double room, travel with the band, and are responsible for a group of 8 to 12 students for the whole trip. Asking to chaperone is a request, not a guarantee: Mr. Parker decides based on the trip's needs, including how many men and women are needed. Until he confirms you, plan to travel on your own (below).

To be confirmed, a chaperone pays in full, is approved as an NHCS Level 2 volunteer by December 10, and signs the [chaperone agreement](/info/carnegie-chaperone-agreement). Ask in the [Family Portal](/portal) under **Carnegie Hall 2027 → Adults coming to New York**.

### Can family come to New York?

Yes, two ways:

- **With the band, traveling on your own:** $2,500 per person in a double, or $2,000 per person in a quad when all four beds are filled (otherwise it becomes a double at $2,500). You get everything the band gets except travel to and from New York. In New York the group walks everywhere except the ferry to Ellis Island. You're welcome to help with students but don't have to.
- **Fully on your own:** no cost through the band. You plan and pay for everything, including your Carnegie Hall ticket. Just let us know you're coming.

Sign up in the [Family Portal](/portal) under **Carnegie Hall 2027 → Adults coming to New York**. Adults may pay in parts; please pay in full by **Monday, November 23**. Last call is the Winter Concert, Tuesday, December 8, and the final cutoff is **Thursday, December 10**. **Every adult payment is non-refundable.** You're added to the travel list only once you've paid in full.

### How do family tickets to the concert work?

Every traveler on the trip has a concert ticket. Family not on the trip buy tickets from the Carnegie Hall box office about six weeks before the concert, around mid-February. The festival guide lists them at $90.

### How does rooming work?

Students room 4 to a room, boys with boys and girls with girls. Adults room 2 to a room, or 4 to a quad when all four beds are filled. The directors are in single rooms.
