## Ashley Bands public information

The Bands of Ashley High School serves students, families, the school, and the Wilmington, North Carolina community. Ensembles include concert bands, the Screaming Eagle Regiment marching band, percussion, jazz, colorguard, and pep band.

Mr. Parker is the Director of Bands. Public contact: robert.parker@nhcs.net or (910) 790-2360. School address: 555 Halyburton Memorial Parkway, Wilmington, NC 28412.

The [band calendar](https://ashleybands.com/calendar) is the official source for dates and times. It provides a subscription link and a one-time .ics download. Subscribe for ongoing updates. Do not substitute an old event date for an upcoming event.

The [Family Portal](https://ashleybands.com/portal) uses a six-digit email code. Use an email already connected to the family. New or unmatched emails can [request access](https://ashleybands.com/portal/request). Direct private student, family, financial-account, and access questions to Mr. Parker.

[AshleyBands Weekly](https://ashleybands.com/newsletter) publishes program news. Current students and families receive the member edition separately. Alumni and community readers may subscribe to the public edition and confirm their email.

For business or family sponsorship levels, benefits, donations, and current giving terms, use [Sponsorship opportunities](https://ashleybands.com/sponsors). Do not quote legacy sponsorship sheets as current offers.

Past spring-trip and InstaRaise instructions are archived. They are not current trip prices, payment instructions, or active fundraising campaigns. If a visitor asks about a trip without naming it, ask which trip they mean. Use the current Carnegie commitment information for Carnegie questions.
