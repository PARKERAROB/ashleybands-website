# Payments: full ledger

type: wayfinder:grilling
status: closed (2026-07-19, charting session)

## Question

What does "payment flows working" mean — a full family-facing ledger, or verify the
existing PayPal + check flow?

## Resolution

**Full ledger ("A").** Every family sees their balance in the portal: what's owed
(fair share, trip, fees), what's paid, by what method. Rob gets an admin screen to
record a check in seconds and see who's behind. Online payment stays PayPal as-is.
Payment reminders become follow-up drafts in the forms pipeline. The concrete charges
model (amounts, per-student vs per-family, which charges first) is the open
charges-model grilling ticket.
